About Me

I’m drawn to projects that feel useful, hands-on, and connected to the real world. I like working on systems where mechanical design, electronics, programming, and power all have to come together.

A lot of my interests come from wanting to build technology that supports sustainability, clean energy, and communities. I’m especially interested in renewable energy, embedded systems, power electronics, and automation.

Outside of school and engineering, I’m into surfing, snowboarding, guitar, and spending time outside. That ocean and outdoor influence is a big part of the style of this website too.
