About Me

I strive to blend engineering with a sustainability-driven perspective, guided by my Navajo heritage and a commitment to building solutions that support both community and environment.

I am especially interested in power systems, embedded hardware, electromechanical systems, and renewable energy technologies where engineering decisions directly affect efficiency, reliability, and scalability. My work focuses on turning concepts into fully tested systems through electrical schematics, embedded firmware, sensor integration, CAD modeling, power distribution, and iterative hardware validation.
