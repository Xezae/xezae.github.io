More About Me

Engineering, the outdoors, endurance, and building meaningful things.

Outside of engineering, a lot of my life revolves around the outdoors, endurance sports, hands-on work, and the people around me. Whether I am surfing, snowboarding, backpacking, competing in triathlons, or working on mechanical systems, I enjoy experiences that challenge me to keep learning and improving. A lot of these experiences shape how I approach engineering — staying adaptable, solving problems calmly, and building systems that have a meaningful real-world impact.

Surfing Along The Coast

I have always enjoyed activities that push me mentally and physically. Surfing, snowboarding and triathlons taught me discipline, consistency, and how to stay focused even when things become uncomfortable. I enjoy setting goals, improving over time, and learning from challenges rather than avoiding them.

Backpacking and mechanic work helped me appreciate simplicity, self-reliance, and understanding how systems function in the real world. I have always enjoyed working with my hands, troubleshooting problems, and learning how things operate beyond theory. A lot of my interest in engineering came from helping my father with construction/mechanical projects from buidling dog houses to working with him on fixing motorcycles, vehicle maitenance, to working on the family project Chevy C10 truck.

Long term, I hope to work on technologies that combine sustainability, embedded systems, power systems, and clean energy while continuing to stay connected to the outdoors and the communities around me. I want to build systems that are both technically meaningful and capable of making a positive real-world impact.
